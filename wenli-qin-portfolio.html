Featured story

The client who taught me to listen

Weimob · a community-industry client

The setup

A community-industry client kept telling us we'd never found their real pain point — and the account had become hard to reach. Then it landed with me.

What I did

I stopped pitching and started listening — really listening, every meeting. I studied similar cases and styles, then designed community campaigns around their needs: activities that grew their subscriptions and community members, and posters they were proud to put their name on.

The result

The client renewed — and more importantly, trusted me. It's the story I tell when someone asks how I work with people.
